      THE PROCESS

      Three steps. One PDF.

      01

      Paste your list

      Use any simple card list or an Archidekt export. Mainboard only; removed cards and maybeboard entries stay out.

      02

      Pick your settings

      Select standard/latest or expensive artwork, your card layout, image quality and trim marks.

      03

      Download and print

      The PDF is assembled locally in your browser. Print on US Letter at 100% scale and trim along the marks.
